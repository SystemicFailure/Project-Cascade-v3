## Findings

- Kenya schistosomiasis (2000s): major drought reduced transmission by killing snails, CONFIRMING that extreme conditions can reset baseline but at high cost
- California cholera outbreaks (2020s): drought forced reliance on unsafe water sources, CONFIRMING resource scarcity drives exposure pathways and disease spike
- Brazil yellow fever outbreak (2026): drought-driven mosquito adaptation enabling pathogen persistence in dry conditions, NEW CASCADE confirmation in real-world field system
- California avian botulism (2024): drought conditions amplified fungal reproduction, creating disease outbreak cascade in wildlife—ecological system-level failure mode
- West Nile Virus amplification via suppressed dragonfly larvae: drought removes biological control predators, enabling mosquito vector multiplication without intervention
- AMOC stability concerns documented: Atlantic meridional overturning circulation showing signs of reduced stability, threatening thermohaline architecture
- "Snow-eater heatwaves" observation: heatwaves consuming snowpack earlier in season, shortening water supply window and creating resource scarcity earlier than historical baseline
